# Assignment: Learning Journal

Write an 8 to 12 sentence journal entry that includes:

- Why you want to learn software development  
- What excites you about this journey  
- What challenges you think you will face  
- How you plan to handle frustration  
- What habits you want to build  
- What success looks like for you in the next 6 to 12 months  

This journal will guide your progress and keep you focused. Revisit it whenever you feel discouraged.
