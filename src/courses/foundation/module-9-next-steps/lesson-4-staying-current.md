# Staying Current in Tech

The tech world moves fast. New frameworks, libraries, and tools are released every day. It can feel like you are standing under a waterfall, trying to drink. This leads to **FOMO** (Fear Of Missing Out).

## The Secret: Fundamentals don't change

While the _tools_ change (React, Vue, Svelte), the _foundations_ stays the same (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, HTTP, Git).
If you master the foundation, you can learn any new tool in a week.

## Curation is Key

Do not try to read everything. Choose a few high-quality sources and ignore the rest.

### Recommended "News" Diet

1.  **Weekly Newsletters:** These are great because someone else filters the noise for you.
    - _JavaScript Weekly_
    - _Python Weekly_
    - _TLDR Tech_
2.  **Podcasts:** Great for passive listening while walking or doing chores.
    - _Syntax.fm_ (Web Development)
    - _CodeNewbie_ (Stories from beginners)
    - _ShopTalk Show_

## The 80/20 Rule

Spend **80%** of your time **doing** (coding, building, fixing debugging).
Spend **20%** of your time **reading/watching** (tutorials, news, blogs).

If you spend 100% of your time reading about code, you aren't a developer; you're a tech spectator.

## Helpful Communities

- [Dev.to](https://dev.to/) - A very friendly blogging community for developers.
- [CodeNewbie](https://www.codenewbie.org/) - Dedicated to supporting people learning to code.
- [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com/) - For finding answers (remember to search first!).
